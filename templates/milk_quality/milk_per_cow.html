One row per farm and day, with a table for each month. Total milk prefers the weighbridge, otherwise the ticket. The 4-day average is the mean of this farm’s last up to four days with milk. Milk per cow is that average divided by cows in milk on that day. The total row adds up Total milk for the month. Cows, the 4-day average, and milk per cow are averages of those days. Fat %, protein %, SCC, Bactoscan, and urea are weighted by that day’s milk.
