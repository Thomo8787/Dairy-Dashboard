About

Holstein bull proofs from AHDB Dairy Breeding. G is marketed genomic; P is proven (UK marketed and top international, one row per bull). Upload the Thomasson bull file to add Custom Index (CI) for each bull. Bulls in that file that are not on AHDB stay on this list until a later AHDB refresh finds them. Use Columns to choose traits and Colours to mark good and poor values. Click a column heading to sort. Tick bulls and choose Add to current bulls to keep a short list for the farm. Current bulls shows that group. Use this list to compare animals — do not republish the dataset.
